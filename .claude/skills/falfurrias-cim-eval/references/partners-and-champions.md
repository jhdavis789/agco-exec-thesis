## Champion routing logic

Read these in order and stop at first match.

1. **Branded food / food manufacturing / protein / snacks / condiments / co-pack** → **Bill Lovette** (primary), with Ed McMahan as IC sponsor and Nick Bueno on market work.
2. **Cybersecurity, MSSP, IT services, AI-enablement, dev infrastructure** → **Joe Oesterling** (operating champion), paired with an FCP or FGP investment partner depending on size.
3. **Fintech, payments, fraud/credit analytics, tech-enabled services to banks/insurers/wealth** → **Wilson Sullivan**.
4. **Software / vertical SaaS / tech-enabled business services, sub-$10M EBITDA, $5M+ ARR** → **Amy Brandt and/or John Comly** (FGP).
5. **Government services / federal digital modernization / national security tech / govtech** → split: software-led to **Amy Brandt / John Comly** (FGP); services-led with $10M+ EBITDA to **Ed McMahan + Nick Bueno** (FCP) until a dedicated govt-services partner is named.
6. **Industrial technology / specialty manufacturing / safety / access / engineered products** → **Ed McMahan** as IC sponsor with **Matthew Kilian** on execution; flag for operating-partner recruitment if no obvious champion exists.
7. **Healthcare IT / services, clinical trial tech, life sciences tooling** → **Ed McMahan + Nick Bueno**; flag operating-partner gap.
8. **Marketing / info services / B2B media / training & education** → varies by check size; route via Nick Bueno to identify the relevant active theme.

## Naming the champion in the IC memo

In the memo, write the champion paragraph this way:

> **Champion:** [Partner Name], [Role]. Rationale: [1 sentence — why this person, citing the specific sector / prior deal pattern].
>
> **Operating partner needed?** [Yes — describe profile / No — covered by existing operating bench].

If no clear champion exists, say so. Falfurrias-style discipline is to *not* force-fit a deal into the wrong partner's coverage.
