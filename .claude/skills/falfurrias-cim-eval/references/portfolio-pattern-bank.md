# Portfolio Pattern Bank

Use these reference deals as comparables when writing the IC memo. The pattern-match line — "this looks like X" — is one of the most powerful framings in a Falfurrias memo because it shortcuts the partners to a known playbook.

Each entry: company → sector tag → what made it a Falfurrias deal → playbook applied.

## Active portfolio (FCP)

### Snak King — packaged food / co-manufacturing (invested Aug 2024)
- One of the largest co-manufacturers of private-label salty snacks in the US.
- **Why Falfurrias:** Branded-food theme, co-manufacturing has both contract durability and pricing leverage in inflationary periods, founder-friendly process.
- **Playbook clue:** Falfurrias likes the co-pack/private-label half of food more than pure CPG with high marketing burn.

### Young American Food Brands (YAFB) — premium protein platform
- Grass-fed and wagyu fresh ground beef, cooked meats, premium frozen burger patties. Founded 1972.
- **Why Falfurrias:** Premium positioning in a commodity-perceived category, multi-channel growth (foodservice + retail), founder-led.
- **Playbook clue:** Premium SKUs within a commoditized category is a recurring Falfurrias pattern (parallels Sauer Brands).

### Oddball — federal civilian digital services (invested Dec 2024)
- Fully remote digital modernization agency serving federal agencies.
- **Why Falfurrias:** Theme = federal shift to civilian digital, multi-year contracts, clearance-mature talent.
- **Playbook clue:** Distributed-workforce delivery model — Falfurrias is comfortable with non-co-located teams in govt services.

### Woven Solutions — national-security software (acquired Aug 2025, 3 add-ons since)
- Reston, VA. Cloud-native software and cybersecurity engineering for national-security customers.
- **Why Falfurrias:** Clearance moat, NatSec budget durability, immediate add-on path (3 closed in months).
- **Playbook clue:** Falfurrias deploys add-on capital aggressively when the platform is sourcing-mature.

### MOXFIVE — cybersecurity incident response
- Founded 2019. Tailored cyber plans + IR services + end-to-end risk management platform.
- **Why Falfurrias:** Cyber-services theme + Joe Oesterling expertise, recurring-engagement model emerging.

## How to use this bank

When evaluating a new CIM:

1. Find the **closest 1–2 comparables** above (by sector + size + model).
2. Quote them in the IC memo: *"This profile is the closest parallel we have to [Sauer Brands / Crosslake / Oddball], where the playbook was [X]."*
3. Note where the new deal **diverges** — different = potentially better or worse, not neutral.
4. If nothing in the bank is a clean comp, that's a yellow flag: the firm's existing operating bench may not cover it.
