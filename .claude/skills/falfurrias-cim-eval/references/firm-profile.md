# Falfurrias Capital Partners — Firm Profile

## Snapshot

- **HQ:** Charlotte, NC
- **Founded:** 2006
- **Founders:** Hugh McColl Jr. (former Chairman/CEO, Bank of America); Marc Oken (former CFO, Bank of America); Ed McMahan (Managing Partner)
- **AUM:** ~$4.0B raised across 8 funds since inception
- **Active capital:** ~$1.75B currently being invested across FCP VI ($1.35B) and FGP I ($400M)
- **Stage:** Lower middle market, control-oriented (FCP) and growth control/large-minority (FGP)

## Two strategies — know which one a CIM fits BEFORE evaluating

### Falfurrias Capital Partners (FCP) — flagship buyout
- **Fund VI:** $1.35B, closed March 2025, oversubscribed
- **Target EBITDA:** $5–60M
- **Position:** Exclusively control
- **Sectors:**
  - Government & business services
  - Food manufacturing
  - Industrial technology
- **Examples:** Snak King, Young American Food Brands, Oddball, Woven Solutions, SixAxis, MOXFIVE, CIH, Sauer Brands (exited 2025), Industry Dive (exited)

### Falfurrias Growth Partners (FGP) — growth equity arm
- **Fund I:** ~$400M, launched 2023
- **Target EBITDA:** $1–7M (and at least $5M ARR)
- **Check size:** $20–50M per transaction
- **Position:** Control OR large minority
- **Sectors:**
  - Software & business services
  - Government technology
  - Fintech
  - Information services
- **Bias:** Cash-flow positive companies with double-digit growth
- **Examples:** Neighborly Software, NPI, Crosslake (continuation vehicle 2026)

**Routing rule:** If a CIM is sub-$5M EBITDA AND pure-SaaS / tech-enabled services, it's FGP. If it's $10M+ EBITDA with physical product, services, or asset-intensity, it's FCP. Gray zone $5–10M: check ARR — recurring revenue tilts FGP, project/transactional revenue tilts FCP.

## The "Industry First" doctrine

Falfurrias's defining feature is a research-led approach branded **"Industry First."** Read the firm's own framing carefully — every IC memo will be judged against it:

> "At Falfurrias, it all starts with a thesis: the best way to help you grow your business is to know your business."

Operational interpretation:
1. **Theme-first sourcing.** They identify durable growth markets, build a thesis, *then* source companies that fit. CIMs that arrive without an obvious connection to a stated theme are downgraded.
2. **Operating partners co-build the thesis.** "Renowned leaders" from each industry are brought in as partners. The investor and the operator co-own the playbook (e.g. Bill Lovette for packaged food — former Sauer Brands Chairman and Pilgrim's Pride CEO).
3. **Concentrated portfolios.** Few deals per fund, deep work per company.
4. **Strategy & Market Insights team.** In-house research function (led by Nick Bueno, ex-McKinsey) develops/substantiates theses and runs commercial diligence.

## What attracts Falfurrias (pattern, not rule)

Cross-cutting characteristics that appear in nearly every Falfurrias deal:

1. **Founder- or family-owned** with established trajectory; institutional capital is the unlock, not the rescue.
2. **Mission-critical to customers** (govt agencies, FIs, healthcare providers, ingredient buyers).
3. **Niche category leadership** rather than horizontal scale plays.
4. **Tech-enabled services** more than pure SaaS or pure services — the blend is the moat.
5. **Recurring or contracted revenue** (subscriptions, multi-year govt contracts, repeat-purchase consumables).
6. **Add-on optionality** — most Falfurrias platforms do 2–5 add-ons (e.g. SixAxis + Upside Innovations; Sauer Brands + Mateo's Salsa).
7. **Operational uplift available** — pricing, mix, channel expansion, systems modernization.
8. **Sector tailwind** that can be sourced and quantified externally (USDA data, federal contracting, healthcare spend, etc.).

## What disqualifies (or strongly downgrades)

- Sub-scale (FCP <$5M EBITDA, FGP <$1M EBITDA) with no near-term path to scale
- Pure consumer-direct without a B2B anchor
- Heavy capex / project-based with no recurring layer
- Founder unwilling to roll equity or exit immediately at close
- Customer concentration >40% with single buyer, no multi-year contract
- Cyclical demand without a structural tailwind
- Regulated retail / lottery / cannabis / firearms (no public evidence of these in portfolio)

## Geography

US-focused. No publicly visible non-US platforms. Treat non-US targets as out-of-scope absent specific signal.
