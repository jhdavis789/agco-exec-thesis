# Sector Fit Rubric

For each of Falfurrias's active themes, what makes a target a *fit* vs. a *stretch*. Use this rubric in the IC memo's "Strategic Fit" section. Score each as **Fit / Stretch / Off-thesis**.

## Packaged food / food manufacturing (FCP)

**Champion:** Bill Lovette + Ed McMahan.

**Fit signals:**
- Premium positioning in a commoditized category (Sauer Brands template)
- Co-packing or private-label with diversified customer base
- Branded with multi-channel distribution (foodservice + retail)
- Founder/family-owned, multi-decade operation
- Adjacent SKU expansion runway (e.g. condiment → salsa → dressing)
- Clean ingredient or "better-for-you" angle that has data behind it (not just claim)

**Stretch signals:**
- Heavy DTC dependency
- High marketing burn
- Commoditized only (no premium tier)
- Single-customer (one big retailer)

**Off-thesis:**
- Restaurants / hospitality
- Pre-branded ingredient producers without consumer pull-through
- Alcoholic beverages (no public evidence in portfolio)

## Government & business services / federal civilian / national security (FCP)

**Champion:** Ed McMahan + Nick Bueno (currently). Future-state: dedicated govt-services partner.

**Fit signals:**
- Multi-year contract vehicle (IDIQ, OTAs, GWAC)
- Clearance-mature workforce (SCI/TS bias for NatSec)
- Mission-tech bias (digital modernization, cyber, data) rather than commodity bodyshop
- Past-performance on relevant agencies
- Add-on-friendly platform (proven sourcing of complementary capabilities)

**Stretch:**
- Heavy small-business set-aside dependency that won't survive scale
- Single-agency exposure
- Subcontractor-heavy (low margin, low durability)

**Off-thesis:**
- State & local only at small scale
- Pure staff augmentation

## Industrial technology / specialty manufacturing (FCP)

**Champion:** Ed McMahan + Matthew Kilian.

**Fit signals:**
- Niche category leader with engineered product moat
- Safety/regulatory tailwind (OSHA, ASTM, EU CE)
- Multi-decade installed base supporting aftermarket
- B2B distribution with channel relationships
- Founder/family-owned

**Stretch:**
- Heavily cyclical end markets (construction at peak)
- No aftermarket / parts business

**Off-thesis:**
- Capital-intensive raw materials processing
- Heavy carbon exposure

## Cybersecurity services / MSSPs / IT services (FCP)

**Champion:** Joe Oesterling (operating).

**Fit signals:**
- Services-led with software/platform overlay (MOXFIVE pattern)
- Mid-market or enterprise B2B
- Incident response or managed detection with retained customers
- Compliance-driven demand (PCI, HIPAA, CMMC)

**Stretch:**
- Project-only consulting with no managed service
- Heavy reliance on partner platform (e.g. only one EDR vendor)

**Off-thesis:**
- Pure software cyber startups (Falfurrias does services)

## Fintech / fraud-and-credit analytics / tech-enabled services to FIs (FGP)

**Champion:** Wilson Sullivan.

**Fit signals:**
- FI customers (banks, insurers, asset managers, payment cos)
- Regulated/compliance-adjacent moat
- Vertical SaaS or tech-enabled service with multi-year contracts
- Data network effects (each customer makes the model better)

**Stretch:**
- B2C fintech with high CAC
- Reg-only-arbitrage businesses

**Off-thesis:**
- Crypto/digital-asset native (no public evidence)
- Consumer lending balance-sheet

## Software / vertical SaaS (FGP)

**Champion:** Amy Brandt + John Comly.

**Fit signals:**
- Cash-flow positive
- Double-digit organic growth
- $5M+ ARR, $1–7M EBITDA
- Vertical niche (vs. horizontal)
- Founder-owned, capital-efficient
- High gross retention (>90%), net retention >100%

**Stretch:**
- Burning cash with strong growth (FGP wants cash-positive)
- High concentration in a fragile end market

**Off-thesis:**
- Pre-revenue / pre-product fit
- Horizontal SaaS competing with platform incumbents

## Healthcare IT / services / clinical trial tech (FCP / FGP)

**Champion:** Ed McMahan + Nick Bueno (gap on dedicated healthcare partner).

**Fit signals:**
- Sponsor-funded revenue (pharma, payer) — not patient-direct
- Workflow software in a regulated process
- Real-world data assets

**Stretch:**
- Provider-direct sales with budget unpredictability

**Off-thesis:**
- Therapeutics / device hardware
- Insurance carrier risk

## Information services / marketing / B2B media / training (cross)

**Champion:** routes via Nick Bueno.

**Fit signals:**
- Subscription or membership revenue
- Senior decision-maker audience
- Vertical content depth + data products
- Marketing/intel services attached (Industry Dive pattern)

**Stretch:**
- Pure event-revenue businesses
- Ad-only monetization

**Off-thesis:**
- Consumer media
